The SPI mailbox reaches sensor registers directly. Exposure, trigger period and frame count are not set this way — they have dedicated commands on the Ft+, below.

Camera commands — Ft+ (Port B) only

A single 32-bit word on the OUT pipe: {opcode[31:28], payload[27:0]}. These are the runtime capture controls and they are not available over the UART — the Ft+ has to be stacked.

Hold camera in resetsetopcode 6ms, or 0 = releaseSelf-releasing after the given milliseconds. How the camera is idled for independence tests.

Changing exposure alone changes nothing you can see. Capture is one-shot per bitstream load, and DDR still holds the burst taken at the old value — so a new exposure needs a re-arm (opcode 3) before the next capture reflects it.

Exposure is clamped ≤ 8280 µs at 120 Hz. Past that the frame rate halves; at 8300 µs delivery stops and the sensor wedges until it is reconfigured.
